## Project copyright boundary

This repository is designed to avoid copying standards text into the package. It primarily stores:

- identifiers, versions, levels, titles, and links;
- source and relationship metadata;
- original summaries of review methods and procedures;
- original schemas, validation logic, agents, and reporting code.

Do not replace source-linked summaries with copied specification, translation, checklist, or guidance text unless the applicable terms have been reviewed and the required notices are added.

W3C publishes different kinds of material under different licenses. This project does not claim W3C endorsement, certification, or authorship, and it does not present project-authored procedures as modified W3C specifications.

## WAIC translations and guidance

The package references material published by the Web Accessibility Infrastructure Committee (WAIC), including Japanese translations of W3C documents and guidance for using JIS X 8341-3:2016.

WAIC's translated-document license requires, among other things, a link to the WAIC document, translator attribution, the document status, and a statement that the English W3C document is authoritative. Preserve the translation's own notice and any additional named translators.

- WAIC translated-document license: `https://waic.jp/license-for-translated-documents/`

Do not assume that every WAIC checklist, guideline, article, or non-translation document is governed by the translated-document license. The provenance registry marks WAIC JIS guidance as requiring source-specific review before copying text, tables, or substantial portions.

## Digital Agency accessibility policy

The `jp-public-web` compatibility preset uses the 18 additional WCAG 2.2 A/AA targets published in the Digital Agency accessibility policy for `www.digital.go.jp`.

The Digital Agency copyright policy states that Public Data License 1.0 applies unless otherwise specified. It requires source attribution and, when content is edited or processed, disclosure that editing or processing occurred and identification of the responsible party. It also identifies excluded content and page-specific exceptions.

- Accessibility policy: `https://www.digital.go.jp/accessibility-statement`
- Copyright policy: `https://www.digital.go.jp/copyright-policy`

A project-generated profile, catalog, or report must not be presented as an unmodified Digital Agency publication or as a universal Japanese public-sector standard.

## JIS X 8341-3

This repository does not redistribute the official JIS X 8341-3:2016 standard text. It records identifiers, source links, technical relationships, and original summaries.

Use an authorized route to obtain or view the standard and review the applicable terms before reproducing standard content. Project summaries are not the official JIS text.

## Downstream redistribution checklist

Before packaging the catalog or report data into another product:

1. identify which third-party source records are included;
2. open the exact source and current terms URL;
3. preserve required source, copyright, translator, and document-status notices;
4. disclose edits, translations, or processing where required;
5. avoid implying endorsement, certification, or official status;
6. keep copied standard or translation text out of the artifact unless its use has been reviewed;
7. update `source-provenance.json` when a source, imported field, or terms page changes;
8. obtain qualified legal review when the intended commercial or redistributive use is unclear.
